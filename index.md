## 🛠️ Knowledge Base & Project Logs

* **Virtualization Management:** Proficient in **Oracle VM VirtualBox**, including guest OS deployment (Windows 11 Pro, Windows Server, Linux) and the configuration of **nested hardware virtualization**.
